Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

How DigGems collects, uses, and protects your information / 了解 DigGems 如何收集、使用与保护您的信息

Last updated / 最后更新：September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DigGems ("Find Cute Pets", "找到萌宠", "we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile applications (planned Bundle ID: com.lixiao.game.diggems) and related services.

本隐私政策说明 DigGems（找到萌宠 / Find Cute Pets，「我们」或「本应用」）在您使用移动应用（计划 Bundle ID：com.lixiao.game.diggems）及相关服务时如何收集、使用与共享信息。

1. Information We Collect / 我们收集的信息

DigGems does not require a mandatory account. We may collect:

  • Device information — device model, OS version, language, and settings
    设备信息 — 设备型号、系统版本、语言及基本设置
  • Advertising identifiers — IDFA / GAID when you grant tracking/ads permission
    广告标识符 — 在您授权后用于广告展示与效果衡量
  • Usage & progress data — level progress, session metrics; local progress may sync via cloud services
    使用与进度数据 — 关卡进度、会话指标；本地进度可能通过云服务同步
  • Optional social profile — display name / avatar from Game Center, Play Games, or WeChat when you choose to use them
    可选社交资料 — 您主动使用时来自 Game Center、Play Games 或微信的昵称与头像
  • Leaderboard identifiers — anonymous device / platform IDs used to submit scores
    排行榜标识 — 用于上传成绩的匿名设备或平台 ID

2. Device Permissions / 设备权限

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) / advertising consent — for AdMob ads where applicable
    跟踪/广告同意 — 用于 AdMob 等相关广告
  • Game Center / Play Games / WeChat — optional social features and leaderboards
    可选社交与排行榜能力

We do NOT use your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, photos library, or Bluetooth for core gameplay.

我们不将摄像头、麦克风、精确位置、通讯录、相册或蓝牙用于核心玩法。

3. Third-Party Services / 第三方服务

  • Google AdMob — advertisements
    广告展示
  • Google UMP — privacy/consent dialogs
    广告同意弹窗
  • Apple Game Center / Google Play Games / WeChat — optional profiles & social features
    可选资料与社交能力
  • Cloud backends (e.g. PlayFab / WeChat Cloud / similar) — leaderboards and optional progress sync
    云后端 — 排行榜与可选进度同步
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & Advertising
  • Apple Privacy Policy

4. How We Use Information / 信息用途

  • Display and measure ads / 展示与衡量广告
  • Present consent dialogs / 展示同意弹窗
  • Operate leaderboards and optional progress sync / 运营排行榜与可选进度同步
  • Improve stability and fix bugs / 改进稳定性与修复问题

5. Data Sharing / 数据共享

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with ad, platform, and cloud providers only as needed to operate the App.

我们不会出售您的个人信息。数据可能与广告、平台及云服务提供商共享，且仅限于运营所必需的范围。

6. Your Choices / 您的选择

  • ATT / ads: device Settings → Privacy → Tracking / Advertising
    设备设置 → 隐私 → 跟踪 / 广告
  • Social login: disconnect Game Center / Play Games / WeChat in system settings
    可在系统设置中断开相关社交账号

7. Children's Privacy / 儿童隐私

DigGems is not directed at children under 13.

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。
