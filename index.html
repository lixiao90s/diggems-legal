DigGems

DigGems is a relaxing Queens-style puzzle: place exactly one pet per row, column, and color region — and never let pets touch, even diagonally.

DigGems（找到萌宠）是一款轻松的 Queens 解谜：每行、每列、每个色块恰好一只萌宠，且萌宠不能八邻相邻。

Game Features / 游戏特色

Simple Rules / 规则清晰

One pet per row, column, and color — no 8-direction adjacency.

每行、每列、每个色块一只萌宠，且不能八邻相邻。

Cute Pets / 萌宠棋盘

Colorful boards and adorable animals — every level is a chill brain teaser.

彩色色块与可爱萌宠，每一关都是轻松益智体验。

Leaderboards / 排行榜

Climb progress and weekly challenge boards across platforms.

通关总榜与每周挑战，多端一起冲分。
